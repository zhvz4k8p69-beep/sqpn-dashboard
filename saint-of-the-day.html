The Exaltation of the Holy Cross
Saint Helena's fourth-century excavation in Jerusalem turned up the cross tradition holds Christ died on, and pilgrims were soon lining up to kiss it. Three centuries later Emperor Heraclius won it back from the Persians, but couldn't carry it into Jerusalem until he took off his royal robes and walked in as a barefoot pilgrim.
