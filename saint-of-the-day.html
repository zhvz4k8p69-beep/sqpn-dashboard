Saint Monica
Monica spent decades in prayer and tears for her wayward son Augustine before he converted and was baptized by Saint Ambrose in Milan in 387. Near the end of her life she told him, "Son, nothing in this world now affords me delight... all my hopes in this world being now fulfilled."
