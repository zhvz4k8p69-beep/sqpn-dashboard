Saint Jane Frances de Chantal
Religious
Jane Frances de Chantal was a mother of six who fell into deep grief when her husband was killed in a hunting accident. At 45, guided by Saint Francis de Sales, she co-founded the Visitation order; by her death in 1641 it had grown to 87 convents.
