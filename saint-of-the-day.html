Saint Alphonsus Liguori
Bishop and Doctor of the Church
Alphonsus Liguori earned a doctorate in canon and civil law at the University of Naples at 16, then gave up the practice of law to preach parish missions and found the Redemptorists in 1732. Pius XII named him patron of moral theologians in 1950.
