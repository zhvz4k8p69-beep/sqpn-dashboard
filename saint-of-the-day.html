Memorial of Saint Maximilian Kolbe, Priest and Martyr

Friday, August 14, 2026
Memorial
Saint Maximilian Kolbe
Priest and Martyr
At Auschwitz in 1941, Kolbe volunteered to die in place of Franciszek Gajowniczek, a stranger picked for the starvation bunker who had a wife and children. Guards killed Kolbe with a lethal injection two weeks later, on this date.
