Memorial of Saint Peter Claver, Priest

Wednesday, September 9, 2026
Memorial
Saint Peter Claver
Priest
When Peter Claver made his final Jesuit vows in Cartagena in 1622, he signed his name Petrus Claver, aethiopum semper servus — Peter Claver, servant of the Africans forever. He kept that vow for 40 years, boarding slave ships as they entered port to treat, feed, and baptize the arriving captives.
