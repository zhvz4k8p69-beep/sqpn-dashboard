Saint Andrew Dung-Lac and Companions
Priest and Martyrs
Andrew, a Vietnamese priest ordained in 1823, was beheaded in Hanoi on December 21, 1839. He is one of 117 people martyred in Vietnam between 1820 and 1862, canonized together by Pope John Paul II in 1988.
