Memorial
The Queenship of the Blessed Virgin Mary
Pope Pius XII established this feast in 1954, grounding it in Mary's closeness to Christ's own kingship as Mother of God. The title is older than the feast: Saint Ephrem was already calling her "Lady" and "Queen" back in the fourth century.
