Saint Denis
Bishop and Companions, Martyrs
Denis was sent from Rome to be the first bishop of Paris around the year 250. He was beheaded there during Emperor Valerian's persecution in 258, martyred alongside the priest Rusticus and the deacon Eleutherius.
