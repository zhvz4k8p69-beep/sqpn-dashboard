Saints Cosmas and Damian
Martyrs
Cosmas and Damian were twin physicians in Roman Cilicia who treated the sick without ever taking payment, which is why ancient writers called them "the silverless" — Diocletian had them beheaded around the year 287 for refusing to give up the faith.
