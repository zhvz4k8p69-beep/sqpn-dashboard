Saint Hildegard of Bingen
Virgin and Doctor of the Church
Hildegard of Bingen was a Benedictine abbess who wrote down mystical visions she'd received since childhood, composed music, studied medicine, and confronted Emperor Frederick Barbarossa over his support for antipopes. Pope Benedict XVI named her a Doctor of the Church in 2012.
