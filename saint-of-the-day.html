Memorial of Saint Dominic, Priest

Saturday, August 8, 2026
Memorial
Saint Dominic
Priest
Dominic watched the Church's preachers lose ground to the Albigensian ascetics because the preachers rode fine horses and stayed at the best inns while the heretics lived like beggars. So he ditched the comfort, started preaching on foot with three Cistercians, and ten years of that itinerant work became the Order of Preachers, founded at Toulouse in 1215.
Source: Franciscan Media
