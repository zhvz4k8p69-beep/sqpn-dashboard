Saint Ignatius of Loyola
Priest
A cannonball shattered his leg at the siege of Pamplona in 1521. The recovery — with nothing to read but a life of Christ — turned him toward the faith that produced the Spiritual Exercises and, in 1540, the Society of Jesus.
