Feast of Saint James, Apostle

Saturday, July 25, 2026
Feast
Saint James
Apostle
James was the first of the Twelve apostles to be martyred. Herod Agrippa had him beheaded around AD 44, the earliest recorded apostolic martyrdom (Acts 12:1-3).
